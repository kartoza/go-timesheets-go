# AI Assistant Query Reference

This document catalogs all supported query types for the AI assistant.
It serves as training context for the LLM and as documentation for users.

## Project & Task Matching

These queries help users find the right project/task/activity to log time against.

- Which project for GeoServer Docker?
- Where should I log code review time?
- Which project is for QGIS plugin development?
- What task should I use for documentation work?
- Where do I log meeting time?
- Which activity type for DevOps work?
- What project covers infrastructure maintenance?
- Where should I log training time?

## Personal Time Analysis

### Daily

- How many hours did I work today?
- What did I work on today?
- What has been my most productive day?
- What has been my longest day?
- What is the average number of hours I log per day?
- What is the average log session length for myself?

### Weekly

- Which projects did I work on last week?
- Am I on track this week?
- Am I logging enough hours this week?
- What was my longest week?
- How many hours did I log this week?
- What's my overtime this week?

### Monthly

- What was my longest month?
- How many hours did I work this month?
- Compare my hours across projects this month

### General Period

- Which projects did I work on last month?
- Which project did I spend the most time on?
- Which project have I logged the most time against?
- How much time did I spend on admin last week?
- What did I work on yesterday?
- Total hours last week

## Tracking Quality & Consistency

- How consistent was my timekeeping?
- Do I have any gaps in my timesheet?
- Am I logging time accurately?
- Are my entries rounded or precise?
- How much of my time is retrospectively logged?

## Productivity & Work Patterns

- What's my utilization rate?
- What's my utilization rate this month?
- How much context switching am I doing?
- How many projects do I touch per day?
- Am I doing too much context switching?
- Do I have good focus time?
- What's my overtime this week?
- How is my work-life balance based on logged hours?

## Submissions & Compliance

- Show my unsubmitted entries
- Which entries are pending submission?
- Do I have entries not submitted from last week?

## Comparisons & Trends

### Week-over-Week

- Show me week over week trends
- How do my hours compare week to week?
- Weekly comparison of hours logged

### Project Comparison

- Compare my hours across projects this month
- Which project takes the most time?
- How is my time distributed across projects?
- What is the breakdown by project?

### Team Comparison

- How do I compare to my colleagues on average daily logged hours?
- Who logs the least time?
- Who logs the most different activities?
- What is the average log session length for my colleagues?
- What is the company average of hours logged per day?
- How does my utilization compare to the team?
- Who on the team is logging the most overtime?
- Which team members have timesheet gaps?

## Project & Organizational Queries

- What projects have had the most hours logged against them?
- What tasks and projects never have time logged?
- Which projects are over budget on hours?
- Which projects have the most active contributors?
- What is the average hours per project per week?
- Which activities consume the most team time?

## Activity Breakdown

- What's my activity breakdown?
- How much time do I spend in meetings?
- Breakdown by activity type
- What percentage of my time is development vs admin?

## Advisory & Best Practices

- How should I improve my time tracking?
- What are best practices for timesheet logging?
- How granular should my time entries be?
- Should I track time in real-time or fill in later?
- What utilization rate should I target?
